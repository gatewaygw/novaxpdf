Privacy

Your PDFs stay on your computer. NovaX PDF never uploads them.

What the app contacts

About every six hours the app downloads three small files from this website: latest.json (is there a new version), news.json (a message banner) and ads.json (small text ads). Like any web request, this lets the website host see your IP address. New versions are downloaded from GitHub Releases and checked against a fingerprint before they install.

Ads

Ads are plain text. Clicking one opens its link in your browser. We do not track what you read.

Stored on your computer

Settings such as your brightness level, recent files and dismissed messages are kept locally in the Windows registry.
