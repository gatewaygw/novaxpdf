PDFs that look like paper should: white, sharp, easy to read.

NovaX PDF is a free Windows reader for people who read, mark up and sign a lot of PDFs. Drag the slider to see what Very bright does to a dull scan.

What you get

Bright, clear pages
Normal, Bright and Very bright views, rendered at your screen's full sharpness.
Mark up
Highlight, underline, draw, add text and shapes, then save into the PDF.
Sign
Draw your signature once and place it on any page.
Touch screens
Pinch to zoom, drag to scroll. Everything scrolls, so nothing is ever out of reach.
Tabs and search
Open several PDFs, search inside them, jump with thumbnails and bookmarks.
Stays current
New versions download in the background and are checked before they install.

Install

  1. Press Download for Windows and open NovaXPDF-Setup.exe.
  2. If Windows says "unknown publisher", click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Click through the wizard. It installs to Program Files.
  4. Open NovaX PDF from the Start menu.

Make it your default PDF app

  1. Right-click any PDF in File Explorer.
  2. Choose Open with, then Choose another app.
  3. Pick NovaX PDF and tick Always use this app.
  4. Press OK. The app also shows these steps under Help.

News

    Questions

    Is it really free?

    Yes. The app may show small text ads, which you can hide for the session.

    Does it upload my PDFs?

    No. Your files stay on your computer. The app only fetches update, news and ad notes from this site. See the privacy page.

    How do updates work?

    The app checks a few times a day, downloads a new version, verifies its fingerprint and installs it. Windows asks for permission once.
